System 01 / 06

Two Level Two Post Stack Parking (Dependent System)

This is a two level dependent car parking system; where one car is parked on the ground level and one on the platform (first level). The car on the ground level need to be moved out before parking/removing the car on the raised platform.

  • Silent operation
  • Low maintenance cost
  • Load capacity 1800 Kg · lifting time 35-45 sec

System 02 / 06

Three Level Stack Parking (Dependent System)

This is Parking System offers parking for 3 cars; one on the ground and two on the two upper levels. The car on the ground level will need to be removed before lowering the first level platform; similarly the ground and first level cars will need to be removed before lowering the second level platform.

  • Two platforms per system
  • Four post structure — indoor or outdoor
  • Load capacity 2000 Kg per pallet

System 03 / 06

Four Post Three Level Pit Stack Parking (Pit + Ground + 1)

This is Parking System offers parking for 3 cars; one on the ground and one in the pit and one on the first level. The car on the ground level will need to be removed before lowering the first level platform or lifting up of the platform that goes into the pit.

  • Three platforms per system
  • Dual height limit switches on each machine
  • Pit depth 1900-2100 mm

System 04 / 06

Vertical Horizontal Parking System (Puzzle System)

Multiple parking cars to the limited parking space, save a great deal of room. Except the top floor, each floor has a spare parking space and front-rear guide rails. Each parking space has a pallet which moves horizontally on the guide rail. The pallets on the top floor make up-down movements while the ones on the ground floor make left-right horizontal movement, the other ones can move either left to right or up and Down through continuous space shift, parking and fetching a car can be realized.

  • Elevating speed 6.3 m/min · horizontal 7 m/min
  • Noise ≤ 60 Db(A)
  • Control way: PLC
Enquire

System 05 / 06

Tower Parking

A lifting channel is designed in the middle of the equipment and the parking spaces are divided in two sides. Each parking space has a car carrying pallet which can make a horizontal moving. There is a lifting case designed in the channel can be lifted to the spaces in any layers when dragged by a lifting motor. The car carrying pallet will be drawn or sent to the spaces in both sides by mechanical hands. The car can go out or in when the cases drops to the same level with the ground.

  • Less land area coverage, high usage rate of space
  • Anti-fall, anti-crashing, anti-overloading devices
  • Turn keys, touch screen, RFID card (optional)
